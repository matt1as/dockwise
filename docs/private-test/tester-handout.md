# Dockwise private beginner test

This is a private usability test of Dockwise, a browser trainer for low-speed sailboat docking. It is not a navigation aid or a test of your sailing ability.

## Before you start

- Use a phone or laptop on the same Wi-Fi as the host Mac.
- Open the private URL provided by Mattias. Do not share it publicly.
- You do not need an account. Please do not enter real passwords or personal data.
- Set aside about 20–25 minutes.
- If anything is confusing, that is useful feedback. Do not ask for help immediately unless the page is technically broken.

## Try this sequence

1. Open **Learn** and choose **Momentum and neutral**.
2. Follow the on-screen coach. Notice whether you understand what Ahead, Neutral, and momentum do.
3. Retry once if the lesson fails.
4. Try **Rudder needs flow** and note whether the difference between stationary rudder and rudder with water flow makes sense.
5. Try **Arrive alongside**. Pay attention to when you reduce power and select Neutral.
6. On a phone, try the engine and rudder controls without using a keyboard.
7. If you have time, open **Sandbox** and run one short free-form experiment.

## Please think aloud

Say what you expect to happen before pressing a control. Examples:

- “I expect the bow to move to port.”
- “I am not sure what this target means.”
- “I do not know whether the boat is still moving.”

## End-of-test questions

Use the companion feedback form. Please answer from memory before discussing the simulator with anyone else.

- What was the first thing you understood?
- What was the first thing that confused you?
- Which lesson felt most useful?
- Which lesson felt least believable or least useful?
- Could you tell what to do next without help?
- Did the phone controls feel easy to use?
- Did the boat’s reactions feel plausible enough to practise with?
- What would make you use this again?
- Would you recommend it to another beginner sailor? Why or why not?
- What single change should we make first?
