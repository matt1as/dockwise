Dockwise · Privacy Policy

Your training stays on your device.

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Dockwise does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party telemetry.

On-device storage

Lesson progress, onboarding choices, and scenarios you save are stored locally on your device using the app’s web storage and iOS preferences. This information is used only to restore your Dockwise experience. You can remove it by deleting saved scenarios or uninstalling the app. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup according to your iOS and iCloud settings.

Network access

The iOS app is designed to work offline. Dockwise does not send your simulation activity to us.

Children and sensitive data

Dockwise does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, and does not request health, location, contact, photo, microphone, or other sensitive information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will appear here. Questions may be sent to mattias@nacka.io.
