Dockwise · Support

How can we help?

Email mattias@nacka.io with your device model, iOS version, Dockwise version, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Pause and reset the simulation before retrying a lesson.
  • Confirm your device is running iOS 15 or later.
  • If local progress behaves unexpectedly, reinstalling the app resets all on-device Dockwise data.

Training disclaimer

Dockwise is a qualitative training simulator. It illustrates low-speed relationships between momentum, propeller thrust, prop walk, rudder flow, wind, current, and dock lines using simplified approximations. It is not a certified hydrodynamic model, navigation aid, or substitute for hands-on instruction, vessel-specific procedures, seamanship, or safe judgment on the water.

Privacy

Dockwise works offline and does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Read the complete privacy policy.
